# Arrays Protoype Porject 1 - Night Sky

Alycia Malonga

[View this project online](http://127.0.0.1:5500/Arrays_Intro/Arrays-Prototype-1/index.html)

## Description

Night Sky is a small sketch about stars that are shy of you. The background is an ombre sky that fades from dark navy to a soft pink-purple, with 30 glowing six-pointed stars slowly moving around and twinkling at their own pace. When the mouse gets close to a star, it scatters away before slowly settling back into place. You can also press the spacebar to add a new star or click on one to remove it. I wanted the sketch to feel calm and dreamy, almost like lying outside at night and wishing you could reach out and touch the stars. I like the idea that they move away when you get close but always come back. I also wanted to experiment with using an array and a loop so I could add or remove as many stars as I want while each one still moves, twinkles and reacts to the mouse.
