# Events Prototype 2 - The Stop Light

Alycia Malonga

[View this project online](http://127.0.0.1:5500/Events%20Intro/events-prototype-2/index.html)

## Description
Stop Light is an interactive sketch of a traffic light and a small red car on a road. By clicking anywhere on the canvas, you can switch the light between red and green. When the light is green, the car moves along the road, but when it turns red, the car stops. Once the car reaches the end of the canvas, it comes back on the other side. I wanted to make something simple but fun that shows how one click can change what happens in the whole scene and how the car depends on the traffic light to know when it can move.
